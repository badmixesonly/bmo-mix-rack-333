## What the tool measured, per source

relative to the input. `max GR` is how hard the dynamic bands actually worked
**on that material** — a case reading 0.0 never engaged, so its pair is two
static filters and says nothing about dynamics.

## Before you listen

- **Check the level.** Case 4 renders to −0.0 dBFS on this vocal. Turn down
  before playing it, and keep the same gain across A and B of a pair.
- **Play a pair back to back, not a bank of files.** The question is per case.
- **Do case 6 first.** It is the control: the two renders differ by −32.5 dB
  relative to input, which should be inaudible. If you can tell that pair
  apart, something other than the topology is being heard — a level
  difference, playback order, a click at the file start. Fix that before
  trusting any other row.
- **The order of A and B is shuffled per case**, so a preference for "A" is
  not a preference for one topology. That is the point.

## What the answer decides

Serial was chosen on the measurements (`spec/topology-options.md`) and every
test that can be written is already written: the response is the band curves
added in dB, a low cut still cuts under a boost, static bands do not depend on
their order. What is left is whether the difference matters on real material,
and whether anything about serial is worse.

If serial holds, `DspCore.h`'s `Topology` default stays where it is and spec
C4 is revised in the same commit. **Whether a serial/parallel switch is ever
exposed as a control is a separate question, held open until this pass is
heard** (Frosty, 2026-09-11) — and it is the one thing still keeping DEQ's
panel out of the UI pass.
